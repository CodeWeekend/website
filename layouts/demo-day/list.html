More than a graduation

A room full of next steps.

CodeWeekend began in Kabul in 2014 as a grassroots gathering for developers. More than 100 community events later, we have grown into a Canadian non-profit delivering free, practical technology education for Afghan youth.

Our Web & AI Development Bootcamp is fully live and built around making. Students learn core web technologies, modern AI tools, collaboration, and the confidence to take an idea from a blank screen to a working product.

When is Demo Day?

The date and start time are being finalized. RSVP now and we will send the confirmed schedule, primary timezone, and UTC reference directly to you.

Where does the event take place?

Demo Day is fully online. Confirmed guests will receive the private joining link and simple access instructions by email.

Who can attend?

Registration is open to employers, technologists, partners, mentors, alumni, media, friends, families, and anyone interested in supporting emerging talent.

Is there a cost?

No. Attendance is free, but registration is required so we can plan capacity and send you the secure event link.

Will the event be recorded?

We plan to record the formal program. Recording and photography will follow CodeWeekend's student consent and privacy practices.

What if I have an access need?

Tell us in the RSVP form. We will share available captioning and access arrangements with confirmed guests before the event.

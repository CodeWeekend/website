## What the process includes {#process data-kicker="01 — The process"}

<div>

[Software development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development) includes everything between conceiving the desired software and producing it, sometimes through a planned and structured process.

It can include many activities that result in the software products we use every day:

<ul class="news-hire__skills">
  <li>Research</li>
  <li>New development</li>
  <li>Prototyping</li>
  <li>Modification</li>
  <li>Reuse</li>
  <li>Re-engineering</li>
  <li>Maintenance</li>
</ul>

</div>

</div>

## The same process, everywhere {#everywhere data-kicker="02 — Instructions"}

<div>

Software can be applied to a huge variety of uses and contexts, but its development follows recognizable processes around the world. Code is what makes programs do what they are designed to do, and computers, within the limits of their capacity, apply its instructions exactly as they are laid out.

<p class="news-pull">Code gives computers specific, logical instructions — for any person, in any country.</p>

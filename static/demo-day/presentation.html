Welcome Demo Day 2026

Welcome, everyone.

Thank you to our guests, mentors, partners, employers, friends, and families for joining us. Today we celebrate 25 graduates of the Web & AI Development Bootcamp — and the projects they are ready to share.

This is not only a graduation. It is a live look at what emerging developers can imagine, build, and explain in their own words.

About CodeWeekend Mission

A room full of next steps.

CodeWeekend began in Kabul in 2014 as a grassroots gathering for developers. Today we are a Canadian non-profit delivering free, practical technology education for Afghan youth — with programs focused on women and girls.

The journey Web & AI Development Bootcamp

From curiosity to craft.

Across the bootcamp, students learned by making — strengthening fundamentals, collaborating, shipping practice projects, and growing the confidence to take an idea from a blank screen to a working product.

01

Learn

Core web technologies, modern AI tools, and the habits of professional builders.

02

Build

Hands-on projects that turn lessons into working interfaces, flows, and features.

03

Struggle

Debugging, iteration, and resilience when something breaks — then rebuilding better.

04

Grow

Capstone work that shows how far each graduate has come, in their own voice.

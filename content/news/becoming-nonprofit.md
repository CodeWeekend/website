## Step-by-step learning {#curriculum data-kicker="02 — The curriculum"}

The Bootcamp guides learners from the basics of technology to building real projects they can share proudly — combining guided lessons with hands-on practice, so they gain both technical skills and the confidence to use them.

</div>

<ol class="news-phases news-wide">
  <li><strong>Foundations</strong><p>Computer basics, the internet, Git, and essential digital tools.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Frontend development</strong><p>HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and the React framework — to create modern, user-friendly websites.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Introduction to AI</strong><p>The growing field of artificial intelligence, including how to connect large language models (LLMs) to web applications in practical ways.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Capstone &amp; Demo Day</strong><p>Each student designs and develops her own application, presented at Demo Day to celebrate her progress.</p></li>
</ol>

## Becoming a non-profit {#non-profit data-kicker="04 — A new structure"}

Alongside the launch of this cohort, CodeWeekend is now officially incorporated as a **non-profit society in British Columbia, Canada**.

From the very beginning, CodeWeekend has been a community effort. Becoming a non-profit is not just an administrative step — it reflects the belief and trust of the mentors, volunteers, learners, and partners who have carried us forward.

<p class="news-demo__note">With a non-profit foundation in place, we are ready to grow our impact in Afghanistan, in Canada, and beyond.</p>

</div>

## The people in the room {#mentors data-kicker="05 — Instructors & mentors"}

<div>

This year’s Bootcamp is led by **Jamshid Hashimi**, who guides students through the structured curriculum. Alongside him, a group of dedicated **female mentors from Afghanistan and abroad** join the live sessions to share their professional experiences, answer questions, and encourage learners with their own stories.

This mentorship helps students see what is possible — a reminder that a career in technology is within their reach.

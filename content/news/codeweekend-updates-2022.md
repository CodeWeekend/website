<div class="news-head news-wide">

## The third cohort {#cohort data-kicker="01 — The Bootcamp"}

We organized the third cohort of our coding Bootcamp program, and received over 250 applications.

</div>

<div class="news-cols news-wide">
  <p class="news-pull">It is not a course. It is a Bootcamp.</p>
  <div>
    <p>That is what we tell our students. By the nature of how we designed the program, not everyone gets in, and not everyone graduates.</p>
    <p>The program now runs strongly with <strong>30 students</strong> — for sure the best ones out there. By February 2023 they will graduate as full-stack developers, and some have already found jobs and been hired.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="news-head news-head--top news-wide">

## Run by instructors and mentors {#mentors data-kicker="02 — The people"}

<div>

CodeWeekend is run by instructors and mentors. This year we worked with an incredible number of them, and they brought a lot of value to our students and to the wider CodeWeekend community.

Their dedication — many of them volunteers contributing hours alongside full-time jobs — is what makes the program work.

</div>

</div>

## What’s ahead in 2023 {#ahead data-kicker="04 — What’s next"}

We are planning to hire full-time employees working in many areas of CodeWeekend. After more than eight years of operation, we think we are ready for that.

As our third cohort finishes, we will start the fourth cohort of the CodeWeekend Bootcamp. With full-time staff looking after many parts of our delivery and the program, we will be in a much better position to scale our work.

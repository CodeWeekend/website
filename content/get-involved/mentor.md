## A note on the 2025 cohort

The 2025 Web & AI Bootcamp is for **women and girls in Afghanistan**. We've intentionally built the mentor pool around **female mentors from Afghanistan and abroad** so participants see what is possible — and have someone in the room who has navigated the path they are walking.

If you're a working female developer interested in mentoring this cohort or future ones, **we particularly need you**. If you identify otherwise, you're still warmly welcome to mentor — see below.

## What mentoring looks like

Mentoring is a roughly two-hour weekly commitment, with a cohort-length minimum (twelve weeks for the 2025 program). The two hours typically break down as:

- **One hour: 1:1 with a student.** A scheduled call where you walk through their code, talk about what they are stuck on, and answer questions about the industry they're trying to break into.
- **One hour: cohort availability.** You're in the cohort's chat during the week. When students post questions in your area of expertise, you weigh in. You might review a pull request or two.

You don't run the curriculum. The instructors do that. Your job is the human-scale support that makes a curriculum stick.

## Who we look for

- **Working developers**, ideally with at least 3 years of professional experience.
- **Patience for "wait, what's a closure?" questions.** Some of our participants are weeks into their first programming language.
- **Reliability.** A weekly hour with a student matters more than a sporadic five hours. We need people who show up consistently for the length of a cohort.
- **Cultural humility.** Most of our participants are Afghan, often with very different lived experiences than the average mentor. Mentors who can listen and learn alongside teaching do the best work.

## What we provide

- A short onboarding (about 90 minutes total) covering the curriculum, the cohort culture, and how the 1:1 sessions are structured.
- A community of other mentors. Most mentors say the side benefit they didn't expect was meeting other engineers from outside their company.
- The most direct possible impact your spare hours can have on someone else's life.

## Apply to mentor

We recruit mentors before each cohort begins.

[Email mentors@codeweekend.net](mailto:mentors@codeweekend.net) — tell us your background, what languages and frameworks you're strongest in, and how many hours a week you can give. We'll respond within a few days.

If you want to talk to a current mentor before applying, say so in your email and we'll connect you with someone in your area of expertise.

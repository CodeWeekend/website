## Linda Norgrove Foundation

For the 2026 Web & AI Development Bootcamp, the [Linda Norgrove Foundation](https://lindanorgrovefoundation.org) funded **30 full scholarships** and **24 internet packages** for women and girls in Afghanistan.

## Hackajob

In 2022, [Hackajob](https://hackajob.com) funded scholarships for **20 women** in the coding bootcamp. Its team also organized fundraising in support of developer education in Afghanistan.

## Scrimba

[Scrimba](https://scrimba.com) supported remote learning in 2021 and provided **one year of premium course access to 100 learners** in 2022.

## RapidIteration

[RapidIteration](https://rapiditeration.com) has supported CodeWeekend's community, technology work, and earlier website delivery.

Partnership descriptions are tied to the years and activities documented in CodeWeekend's archive; they do not imply a current funding agreement. [Partner with CodeWeekend](/get-involved/partner/) to support a future cohort.

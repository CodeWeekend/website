More than a graduation

A room full of next steps.

CodeWeekend began in Kabul in 2014 as a grassroots gathering for developers. More than 100 community events later, we have grown into a Canadian non-profit delivering free, practical technology education for Afghan youth.

Our Web & AI Development Bootcamp is fully live and built around making. Students learn core web technologies, modern AI tools, collaboration, and the confidence to take an idea from a blank screen to a working product.

The programme

Come curious. Leave connected.

This is not a slide-deck graduation. It is a live look at what 20+ emerging developers can imagine, build, and explain in their own words — with the full cohort introduced below.

  1. 01

    Live project demos

    More than twenty graduates present their strongest Web and AI projects, followed by questions from the audience and judges.

    20+ presentations
  2. 02

    Audience Choice

    Guests vote live across five awards — from the strongest overall project to innovation, technical craft, design, and real-world impact.

    Five awards
    1. 01 Best Overall Project The strongest complete project — idea, execution, usefulness, craft, and presentation.
    2. 02 Most Innovative Project The most original idea — creativity and a fresh approach to the problem.
    3. 03 Best Technical Execution The strongest build — clean functionality and technology used well.
    4. 04 Best User Experience & Design The best experience — usability, clarity, and thoughtful design.
    5. 05 Best Real-World Impact The strongest potential impact — useful now, meaningful beyond Demo Day.

    Awards & recognition

    Community applause

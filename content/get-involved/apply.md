## Future cohorts

Applications are not currently open through a public form. CodeWeekend will publish the next cohort's eligibility, schedule, curriculum, available scholarships, and application deadline before accepting submissions.

To register your interest, email [hello@codeweekend.net](mailto:hello@codeweekend.net?subject=Future%20cohort%20application) with:

- Your name and current location
- The program you are interested in
- Your current level of technical experience
- Any accessibility, connectivity, or equipment support you may need

Do not send identity documents or other sensitive personal information by email. CodeWeekend will explain any required verification through the official application process.

Review the [Web & AI Development](/programs/web-and-ai/) and [Full Stack Development](/programs/full-stack/) program pages before registering your interest.

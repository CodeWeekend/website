## Get in touch

We'd love to hear from you. Whether you're interested in mentoring, hiring a graduate, sponsoring a scholarship, partnering with the program, or applying to a future cohort — drop us a line.

### General inquiries

[hello@codeweekend.net](mailto:hello@codeweekend.net)

### Partnerships & sponsorship

If your organization is interested in supporting the next CodeWeekend cohort with scholarships, internet support, laptops, or general operational funding, we'd love to talk.

[partnerships@codeweekend.net](mailto:partnerships@codeweekend.net)

### Hiring our graduates

If you're hiring junior or mid-level developers, designers, or interns, we can introduce you to graduates who are ready to start. Free for both sides — we want our graduates working at good companies.

[hiring@codeweekend.net](mailto:hiring@codeweekend.net) · or [tell us about your role](/get-involved/hire/)

### Mentor inquiries

Are you a working developer with a few hours a month to give? Mentoring is the most direct way to change a learner's trajectory.

[mentors@codeweekend.net](mailto:mentors@codeweekend.net) · or [apply to mentor](/get-involved/mentor/)

### Press and media

For press, interviews, or media inquiries:

[press@codeweekend.net](mailto:press@codeweekend.net)

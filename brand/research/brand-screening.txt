DESIGN DECISION
Keep the approved W. Use the full codeweekend wordmark at first encounter
and the supplied Ink-backed avatar for small profile uses. Do not rely on
orange alone for recognition. Avoid imitating diagonal, brush-like W forms.
A 12-unit cursor notch was compared at 16, 24, 32, 64 and 180 px widths.
It becomes less than one pixel wide at 16 px and is an unreliable identity
detail. The comparison is in notch-comparison.svg/png; it is NOT a logo master.
Neither this decision nor a minor notch establishes legal distinctiveness.
Before a major print or merchandise run, obtain professional name and design
clearance in the intended markets. Existing name use deserves explicit review.

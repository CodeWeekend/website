## Three ways to back this work

CodeWeekend is free for participants because other people make it possible — funders like the [Linda Norgrove Foundation](https://lindanorgrovefoundation.org), mentors who give a few hours a week, and companies that hire our graduates. There are three direct paths to be one of those people.

The cards below describe each path. Each one has a dedicated page that explains exactly what it asks of you, what it commits in return, and how to start.
